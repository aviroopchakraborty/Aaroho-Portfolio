AAROHO

THE SOUND OF THE JOURNEY

Aaroho is a contemporary independent band from Kolkata, built around a simple idea — music should feel alive.

Our music brings together the energy of rock with elements of folk, funk and experimentation. We enjoy exploring different textures and arrangements while keeping the human element of live performance at the centre.

From intimate performances to full-scale stages, our goal remains the same: to connect with the audience through music.

Aaroho is not defined by a single genre. It is defined by a journey — one song, one stage and one experience at a time.
